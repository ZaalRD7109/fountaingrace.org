How to use this well (read me first)

It is a mirror, not a verdict. A result describes a person's default pattern. It does not decide their future, their calling, or their character. God does.

Never label people out loud. Say "you are so good with new people", never "you are such a 2". Use the knowledge to serve, not to sort.

Never let it block or excuse. No type is banned from any role, and no type excuses sin or laziness. It shows the likely fit, that is all.

It serves prayer, never replaces it. Pray over placements and people the same as always. This just gives you better questions to pray with.

Keep results private. Personality data is personal information. Only the pastoral team sees this page. Never read someone's result to the congregation.

Let people disagree. If someone says their result does not feel true, believe them and talk. The conversation is worth more than the score.
