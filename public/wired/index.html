How to use this

Read it twice. Notice what feels true and what does not. Both are worth knowing.

Before big decisions, come back to your question above. Ask it, then pray. It helps you hear whether fear is choosing or wisdom is.

Catch your pressure pattern. The first time you notice it happening in real life and pause, the tool has done its job.

Try serving where you shine. Tell the team you want to try one of those areas. You will feel the difference between serving from duty and serving from design.

Hold it loosely. This is a mirror, not a prophecy. God is still growing you, and He is not limited by any type.
